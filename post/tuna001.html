#TUNA001

Proto-Tuna — ANZOL

May 6th, 2026

Hello! Today I am bringing you a PROTO-TUNA post!
I'm going to do a little feelsy yapping about the origins of this game, and more importantly share some pictures from the very beginnings of this project.

This game was a pet project of mine before we decided to mainline it, and it had recently 180'd into being at all about what it is now. The in-house name of this game had been, and still is,
ABANDONWARE FISHMARKET.
See, ABANDONWARE used to be a really cursed concept. It was the fermented result of yet another cursed game concept I had many years previous, which came from a very unhappy place.
Then, shortly before the OSC team took the project and ran with it, I had a creative breakthrough. I realized I could process those same negative things through creating a positive piece that manages to exist in a space without any of those things, instead of making a negative piece that is inundated in those things. I do think there is a lot of validity and power to processing bad things by making art that faces them directly, but in this specific case, I felt that it was important and awesome to prove to myself and to the world that a piece can exist without certain things being inherent to them.
Upon this realization, I tossed out all designs and preproductions and started over! Actually, I did keep some documents:

  1. A list of squid species I'd like to make characters of, with some complimentary pictures, comparative scientific stats and esoteric flavorings,
  2. An unintelligable and altogether unscholarly document of personal research notes on various marine topics,
  3. Some downloaded articles with sick titles like "Spectral discrimination in color blind animals via chromatic aberration and pupil shape"

Around this time, a close friend of mine had asked me how I approach game design. I had no clue... At the time I was like, "I'm gonna make this whole pet project for them and give it to them on their birthday as a ridiculous way to answer someone's question."

Here's the picture I drew in bed on my phone the night before that aforementioned creative breakthrough. Recognize them? Proto-Abandon...
(I recommend having a drawing app on your phone, just to catch your late night ideas.)

The next day I made this model of, at the time, simply "Box Squid"
Ah man, I felt stoked. I showed OSC being like "look look look look look look." I'm not a 3D artist, or atleast I wasn't before this project, so it was really exciting to make a thing, and I loved it!!! It's so crude and simple but I felt like it had the soul of what I wanted to see. An inherently positive design.
ABANDONWARE was already a thing we would talk about and scheme about, so I had a lot to say about this creative breakthrough as well. And thus, we started scheming on what is now O For Tuna!!
The end!
